## 1. Classification & Provenance Taxonomy

Every salary observation in the JOBINTEL 2.0 registry is assigned a definitive **Provenance Classification**:
- **`OFFICIAL_PUBLISHED`**: The reported wage value is published verbatim in an official government statistical agency release (e.g., US BLS OEWS May 2023).
- **`OFFICIALLY_DERIVED`**: The reported value is derived from official statistical data using a standardized, transparent arithmetic conversion factor (e.g., standard full-time 2,080 annual hours, 52-week ordinary time earnings, or 12-month statutory gross wage).
- **`JOBINTEL_MODEL_ESTIMATE`**: The reported value is generated by JobIntel's calibrated predictive wage models and vacancy distributions, where national statistical surveys do not publish granular 4-digit occupational wage tables.
- **`SUPPRESSED`**: Official statistical figures exist but are withheld from public endpoints by automated legal/governance gates (e.g., UK ONS ASHE fail-closed HTTP 503).
- **`NOT_PUBLISHED`**: The specific metric (e.g., P10 or P90 extreme tails) is not compiled or published by the reporting statistical authority and is strictly recorded as `null`.
