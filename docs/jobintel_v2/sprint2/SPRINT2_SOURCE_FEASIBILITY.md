## Evidence and interpretation

- ASHE Table 15 describes paid hours and weekly/hourly/annual earnings for UK employees by sex and full-/part-time, region, and four-digit SOC. Its current page identifies 2026 provisional data and also carries a delay notice; this discrepancy makes the precise selectable edition a release gate. [ONS ASHE Table 15](https://www.ons.gov.uk/employmentandlabourmarket/peopleinwork/earningsandworkinghours/datasets/regionbyoccupation4digitsoc2010ashetable15) · [2026 ASHE bulletin](https://www.ons.gov.uk/employmentandlabourmarket/peopleinwork/earningsandworkinghours/bulletins/annualsurveyofhoursandearnings/2026)
- ASHE is survey estimates for employee jobs, not all workers or individual-level salary records. The methodology and interpretation guide explain pay-period, measure, population and quality qualifications; gross earnings, basic earnings, mean and median remain separate concepts. [ASHE methodology](https://www.ons.gov.uk/employmentandlabourmarket/peopleinwork/earningsandworkinghours/methodologies/annualsurveyofhoursandearningsashemethodologyandguidance) · [Guide to interpreting ASHE estimates](https://www.ons.gov.uk/employmentandlabourmarket/peopleinwork/earningsandworkinghours/methodologies/guidetointerpretingannualsurveyofhoursandearningsasheestimates)
- SOC 2020 is ONS’s current national occupation classification. Official materials include a hierarchy and coding index, but index title matches are candidate coding evidence and are not guaranteed exact mappings for ambiguous user-entered job titles. [SOC 2020 overview](https://www.ons.gov.uk/methodology/classificationsandstandards/standardoccupationalclassificationsoc/soc2020) · [Volume 2 coding rules and conventions](https://www.ons.gov.uk/methodology/classificationsandstandards/standardoccupationalclassificationsoc/soc2020/soc2020volume2codingrulesandconventions) · [Volume 1 structure](https://www.ons.gov.uk/methodology/classificationsandstandards/standardoccupationalclassificationsoc/soc2020/soc2020volume1structureanddescriptionsofunitgroups)
- The ONS labour-demand publication is a large, periodic aggregate output; its methodology describes Textkernel online-ad sources, filtering, deduplication and occupation/geography assignment. Advert counts measure online postings under that method, not hires, all vacancies or employment growth. [ONS Labour Demand dataset](https://www.ons.gov.uk/employmentandlabourmarket/peopleinwork/employmentandemployeetypes/datasets/labourdemandvolumesbystandardoccupationalclassificationsoc2020uk) · [ONS Textkernel methodology](https://www.ons.gov.uk/employmentandlabourmarket/peopleinwork/employmentandemployeetypes/methodologies/measuringlabourdemandvolumesacrosstheukusingtextkerneldatauserguide)
- ONS dataset pages generally state OGL v3.0 except where noted. The ONS reuse/terms pages preserve third-party and data-protection caveats. A page-level OGL notice is evidence for the published ONS item, not a blanket license for underlying third-party data. [ONS terms](https://www.ons.gov.uk/help/terms-conditions) · [ONS licensing](https://www.ons.gov.uk/methodology/geography/licences/) · [GOV.UK OGL reuse guidance](https://www.gov.uk/help/reuse-govuk-content)
- Companies House provides a public data API and monthly bulk company data, including company type/status, registered address and SIC. Its own guidance says public-register reuse remains the reuser’s responsibility under applicable law. It requires API authentication and has rate limits; registered office is not proof of an operating office or hiring. [Companies House API overview](https://developer.company-information.service.gov.uk/get-started) · [API developer guidelines](https://developer.company-information.service.gov.uk/developer-guidelines) · [Companies House data products](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/companies-house-data-products) · [Companies House personal information guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/your-personal-information-on-the-companies-house-register)

## Recommended selection for a future approved implementation

1. **Candidate salary source:** one verified edition of ONS ASHE Table 15 or a more suitable ASHE table only after comparing exact variable coverage. Select a single compatible reported measure initially (recommend full-time median gross annual earnings if present and not suppressed), preserve the published value/definition, and expose only aggregate source estimates. Confirm the actual 2026 publication state and measure column before acquisition. `REVIEW_REQUIRED` remains the source status.
2. **Occupation base:** SOC 2020 official structure and descriptions; coding index may support search candidates but requires human review/ambiguity handling. Confirm exact selected asset and reuse notice. `REVIEW_REQUIRED` remains.
3. **Defer labour-demand data from the first vertical slice.** It adds 47.7 MB download/processing weight and vendor-derived methodology/licensing review while not supporting the salary outcome. Reconsider only after an exact-file terms review and approved scope. `REVIEW_REQUIRED` remains.
4. **No job ad scraping.** No employer/source data is authorized or acquired by this document.

## Legal and operational gate

Before any acquisition or public exposure, record the exact URL, selected edition, downloaded filename, checksum, embedded workbook notes, applicable reuse/attribution statement, intended transformation/display, and reviewer decision in the source catalog. Keep status `REVIEW_REQUIRED` until that record is complete. If any term excludes the intended use, do not ingest or expose that source. This is a technical feasibility assessment, not legal advice or a substitute for approval.
