For an occupation-to-region comparison screen, this report defines “sufficient regional coverage” as numeric in at least 8 of 11 regions. This leaves 91 occupations (22.1% of unit groups); only 34 (8.3%) are numeric in all 11. The threshold is a transparent analytical filter, not an ONS quality standard and not proof that differences are statistically significant. A regional occupation-profile screen of at least 70% numeric among all 412 codes is met by **no region**.

`x` is explicitly an unreliable estimate (CV >20%); the 2,818 suppressed cells are not zero. Blanks are undefined in the workbook notes and are not assumed to be `x`; they account for 375 further unavailable cells. Numeric-only publication will be selected toward occupations/regions with sufficient precision. Because the missingness is structured and uneven, complete-case rankings and summaries would not represent the full occupation-region universe. The data does not identify the salary direction of missing values, so no high/low bias direction is asserted.

The complete 91-code comparison screen and the full list of occupations with `x` in at least 6 of 11 regions are appended below for exact identification. The 6-of-11 “mostly suppressed” threshold is descriptive and chosen for this report, not an ONS rule. The audit script also outputs all 412 occupation-level numeric/`x`/blank counts.

## Reference period and temporal coverage

All included cells are from **ASHE 2025 revised**. Annual gross pay is the distinct annual measure for the tax year ending 5 April 2025 and eligible adult-rate employees in the same job over one year. This workbook slice provides no region-specific time dimension: temporal trend, year-over-year rank, and revision-change analyses cannot be derived from these 4,532 cells. Weekly and hourly salary measures are separate Table 15 files and are outside this report’s count denominator.

## Reproduction

From the repository root, run:

```powershell
python docs/jobintel_v2/sprint2/ashe_coverage_audit.py
```

The script prints JSON containing region, occupation, major-group, and major-group-by-region coverage and reads without modifying the raw data. It uses the archive at `data/raw/ashe_table15_2025_revised/`, the selected 15.7a estimate and 15.7b CV files, and the official ONS SOC 2020 structure workbook. Denominators, marker handling, CV bands, and analyst screening thresholds are defined in the script and in the companion [data quality report](</E:/Job Market/docs/jobintel_v2/sprint2/SPRINT2_ASHE_DATA_QUALITY_REPORT.md>).
